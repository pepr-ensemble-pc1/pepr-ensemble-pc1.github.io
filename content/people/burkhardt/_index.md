## About

Jean-Marie Burkhardt is a Professor in Psychology and Ergonomics at Univ. G. Eiffel and a member of the LaPEA (Applied Psych. & Ergonomics) Laboratory. His research interest is twofold : 1) the design of emerging technologies such as Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality and eXtended Reality, to support human activities and collaboration; 2) the psychology of design and creative activities.
He is co-leader of the CATS Targeted Project within the PEPR eNSEMBLE program. He was guest co-editor of the special issues on the quality of collaboration of CoDesign: International Journal of CoCreation in Design and the Arts (2012). He has chaired the French Association for Research in Ergonomics and Ergonomics Psychology (ARPEGE) from 2021 to 2024.
