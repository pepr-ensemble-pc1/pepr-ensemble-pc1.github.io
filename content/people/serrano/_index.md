## About

Marcos Serrano is a Professor at the University of Toulouse and a member of the Toulouse Institute of Computer Science Research (IRIT), where he leads the ELIPSE research team. His research focuses on Human–Computer Interaction (HCI), particularly on the theory, design, and empirical evaluation of ubiquitous interaction. He is co-leader of the CATS Targeted Project within the PEPR eNSEMBLE program, and co-director of the CNRS Research Network on Human–Computer Interaction (GDR IHM).
