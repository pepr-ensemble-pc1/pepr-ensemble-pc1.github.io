## About

Maud Marchal is a Professor in Computer Science at INSA Rennes and a member of the IRISA lab. She is also a junior member of Institut Universitaire de France. Her research focuses on 3D interaction, Extended Reality, Haptics and Physics-based simulations. She is co-leader of the CATS Targeted Project within the PEPR eNSEMBLE program, and co-director of the CNRS Research Network on Computer Graphics, Virtual Reality and Visualization (GDR IG-RV).
