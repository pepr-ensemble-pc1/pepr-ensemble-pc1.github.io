## Abstract

My research interest are in the field of robotic, haptic and virtual reality. I aim at improving interaction in VR with a multimodal approach. I have notably studied visuo-haptic illusions in VR, specifically focusing on modeling the detection of visuo-proprioceptive misalignment. I am currently working on collaborative technical skill learning in virtual reality using a visuo-haptic approach.
